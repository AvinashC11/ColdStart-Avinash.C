# Feature Story

# Problem

Millions of people ignore early warning signs such as:

- High blood pressure
- Smoking habits
- Poor lifestyle
- Family history

As a result, diseases are often detected late.

---

# User Pain

Patients struggle to:

- Understand health risks
- Interpret medical numbers
- Know when to seek help

Doctors face:

- Limited consultation time
- Lack of quick risk summaries

---

# Solution

AI Medical Early Disease Risk Prediction provides:

- Early risk estimation
- Explainable predictions
- Preventive guidance
- Doctor-facing summaries

---

# User Journey

Patient opens app.

↓

Enters health information.

↓

AI predicts risk.

↓

System explains important factors.

↓

Patient receives guidance.

↓

Doctor receives summary.

---

# Impact

Benefits:

- Increased awareness
- Faster screening
- Better preventive behavior
- Explainable AI in healthcare
