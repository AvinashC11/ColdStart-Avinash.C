# 2-Minute Demo Script

## Opening (15 seconds)

"Healthcare issues are often detected late because many people ignore early symptoms and lifestyle risk factors.

Our solution uses AI to estimate disease risk, explain why the prediction was made, and provide preventive guidance."

---

# Step 1: Show Dashboard (15 seconds)

Open the Streamlit dashboard.

Highlight:

- Medical disclaimer
- Professional UI
- Patient form

---

# Step 2: Enter Patient Data (20 seconds)

Fill:

- Age
- BMI
- Blood pressure
- Cholesterol
- Smoking status
- Family history

Click:

**Predict Risk**

---

# Step 3: Prediction (20 seconds)

Show:

- Risk score
- Confidence percentage

Explain:

"The model predicts risk probability using multiple health signals."

---

# Step 4: Explainability (20 seconds)

Open the explanation section.

Highlight:

- Feature importance
- SHAP explanation
- Top contributing factors

---

# Step 5: Recommendations (20 seconds)

Show:

- Patient guidance
- Red-flag alerts
- Doctor mode summary

---

# Closing (10 seconds)

"This prototype combines prediction, explainability, and prevention into a single platform and can evolve into a healthcare decision-support system."
