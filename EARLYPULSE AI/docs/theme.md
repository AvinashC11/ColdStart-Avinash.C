# 🎨 MedAI Theme Guide

## Product Vision

MedAI is an AI-powered preventive healthcare platform designed to provide early disease risk insights through explainable machine learning.

The product experience should feel:

- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Minimal
- Modern
- Medical-grade
- Startup-ready
- User-friendly

**Important Disclaimer:**

This application is a hackathon prototype and is not a certified medical device. Predictions and recommendations are intended for educational and research purposes only and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment.

# ✨ Animation Guidelines

Use subtle animations only:

- Fade-in cards
- Hover effects
- Progress bars
- Smooth transitions

Avoid:

- Flashing elements
- Aggressive colors
- Excessive movement
